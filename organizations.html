What works today, and what does not

Working now

  • A directory of … researchers, searchable by field and country.
  • … curated opportunities open right now, each with an eligibility verdict for your country and a link to the funder's own page.
  • Eligibility checked against the published source for all 250 countries — including the cases where a funder's own list overrides the programme rules.
  • Posted needs reviewed by a curator and published with your organisation named.

Not there yet — say so out loud

  • No organisation accounts. Everything here reaches us by email until the backend is deployed.
  • No verified organisation badges, so nothing on this site proves who anyone is.
  • No paid product and no price list. Nothing to buy, nothing to sign.
  • Contacts are never released without the researcher's consent to that specific request — including to you.
  • Support from public figures is not endorsement by the state. QIST is a community project.
