About QIST

ScienceBridge is a platform of QIST (Qazaq International Science and Technology Association), the network of researchers from Kazakhstan and its scientific diaspora worldwide. Its job is to find people international projects, grants, positions and co-authors — and to check eligibility before anyone spends a week on an application they cannot win.

qista.org · pilot stage

Where the researcher profiles came from

The directory was compiled from public sources: university pages, Google Scholar and open researcher profiles. We store name, title, organisation, city, country, research fields and a link to the public profile. Email addresses are neither stored nor published in the directory.

If you found yourself in the directory and want to correct it, take control of the profile, or have it removed — write to us and we will act on it. No justification is needed: your request is enough.

What we do with your data

  • — Your email is used only for the opportunities digest. We do not pass it to third parties and do not publish it in the directory.
  • — You can unsubscribe from any email; the address is deleted when you do.
  • — We never reveal your contact details to an organisation without your explicit consent to that specific request.
  • — The platform is a QIST community project. Public support from government figures does not mean the platform is run by the state.
